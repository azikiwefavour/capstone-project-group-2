Questions hospitals ask us

Do we have to replace our current hospital system?

No. MedTrail runs on its own in any browser and covers prescribing, verification and the patient record. Keep using whatever you already have for billing, labs and admissions.

What if our internet goes down?

MedTrail is light enough to work on slow mobile data. If the connection drops, it tells staff clearly and syncs changes once it's back.

Does MedTrail check for drug interactions?

Today it flags exact duplicates: the same drug already active for that patient. It supports the clinician's decision and never makes one. Full interaction checking is on our roadmap.

How do patients get access?

Patients sign up free with their email and WhatsApp number, or your team adds them and they get a link to set a password. Patient access is view-only.

How long does setup take?

Register your facility and upload your CAC certificate and operating licence. Once we've verified them, invite your doctors and pharmacists and start prescribing the same day.
